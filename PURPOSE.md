# What NexusHQ is for

NexusHQ was created so a product team can keep its writing, its work, and its conversation in one place. Teams usually split those across a document tool, a task tracker, and a chat app. Context gets lost between them, and an assistant sitting outside the workspace cannot tell what a person is allowed to see.

NexusHQ is one workspace for a company or a team. People in that workspace can:

- Write nested pages together, with comments and a history of earlier versions.
- Track projects and tasks on a board, in a list, or on a calendar.
- Talk in channels, threads, and direct messages.
- Search pages, tasks, and messages they are allowed to read.
- Ask an assistant questions about that same work, and turn notes into tasks or a status report.

Access is decided on the server. An owner, admin, member, or guest only sees the organization’s own data, and a guest only sees what they were explicitly given. The assistant uses that same rule: it does not answer from another team’s pages, tasks, or messages.

The app also covers the parts a hosted team product needs around that work: sign-in, invites, plans, API keys, outgoing webhooks, an audit log, and a platform admin view.
